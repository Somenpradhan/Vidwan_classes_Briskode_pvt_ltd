We are a Genius Course @ work since 2007

Vidwan Classes is the leading coaching institute of Odisha & Eastern India for IIT/JEE, NEET/Medical, KVPY, NTSE and all National & International level Olympiads.

Vidwan Classes marked the humble beginning of an exciting odyssey in 2007 bearing the vision of sculpting the world of education with immortal nerve and commitment to excellence. We were the pioneers in Classroom Coaching in Odisha for IIT-JEE and have achieved phenomenal success year after year.

Over the past 17+ years, more than 15,000 Vidwan alumni have entered premier institutions including top IITs (Kharagpur, Bombay, Madras, Kanpur, Delhi, Bhubaneswar), AIIMS New Delhi, SCB Medical, NITs, and top central universities.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our Vision

To empower every passionate student with world-class competitive exam guidance, bridging the gap between ambition and top national ranks through uncompromised academic ethics.

Our Mission

To maintain small batch sizes, offer daily individual doubt desks, provide rigorous test series analytics, and ensure 100% coverage of fundamentals to advanced rank level questions.

Academic integrity, continuous innovation in pedagogy, student-centric mentorship, zero-compromise quality, and nurturing holistic analytical thinking.
